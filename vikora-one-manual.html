1. Sign in and navigate

  1. Open the official Vikora URL supplied by your administrator. The project README currently identifies vikora-one.onrender.com as a test deployment.
  2. Use /login to sign in. On a fresh, migrated, empty database only, the first administrator is created through /signup.
  3. After the first account exists, self-registration closes; an administrator manages additional accounts.
  4. Use the navigation items available to your role. If access is denied, ask the administrator to check permissions rather than bypassing the restriction.

Security: Never include passwords, one-time codes, session cookies, private keys or license codes in screenshots or support messages.

2. Sales, billing and invoicing

Verified routes: /quotations, /sales-orders, /sales-orders/[id], /sales-orders/[id]/edit, /invoices, /documents, /payments.

Quotation / order review

  1. Open Quotations or Sales Orders from the application navigation.
  2. Create or open the relevant record using the controls shown in the deployed release.
  3. Check customer, item/service lines, quantities, rates, tax, dates, currency and totals.
  4. Review the saved detail page and available print view before sharing the document.

Invoice and payment checks

  1. Open the invoice/document register and select the relevant record.
  2. Verify entity, customer, invoice number/date, lines, tax breakdown, total and payment terms.
  3. For payments, confirm the linked party/document, amount, date, method and reference against bank/cash evidence.
  4. If an invoice has already been issued or reported, ask the finance administrator before editing or replacing it.

Do not assume a quotation is an invoice, or that saving a document automatically posts to the ledger. Confirm the exact lifecycle in your current release.

3. Purchase and supplier workflows

Verified routes: /purchase-orders, /purchase-invoices, /goods-receipts, /vendors, /vendor-outstanding, /vendor-payments.

  1. In Purchase Orders, confirm vendor, items/services, quantities, agreed rates, taxes and expected delivery.
  2. In Goods Receipts, compare delivered quantities and condition with supplier documents; investigate shortages, excess or damage before confirmation.
  3. In Purchase Invoices, check supplier identity, invoice number/date, purchase reference, line totals, tax and duplicate-invoice risk.
  4. Reconcile vendor balances with statements and payment evidence.

Do not assume a goods receipt automatically creates or posts a purchase invoice; verify the deployed workflow.

4. Inventory, stock and warehouse

Verified routes: /inventory-items, /stock-inventory, /stock-inventory/dashboard, /stock-inventory/transactions, /stock-inventory/import, /stock-inventory/adjustment, /stock-inventory/adjustment/history, /stock-ledger, /stock-transfers, /stock-reorder, /stock-valuation, /material-requirements, /material-issues, /material-returns.

  1. Confirm the correct item and location/warehouse on screen.
  2. Compare the displayed quantity with a physical count and source documents.
  3. Review recent receipts, issues, returns, transfers and adjustments.
  4. Use an approved adjustment workflow only when a correction is supported by evidence; record the reason.
  5. Recheck stock ledger and valuation after correction, where applicable.

Do not use adjustments to conceal a missing transaction. Test imports with a small non-sensitive sample first and preserve the original file.

5. Accounts and financial reports

Verified routes: /expenses, /expense-categories, /general-ledger, /journal-entries, /trial-balance, /profit-loss, /reports/balance-sheet, /reports/customer-outstanding, /reports/customer-collection, /vendor-outstanding, /financial-years.

  1. Confirm the company, financial year and reporting period.
  2. Check that sales, purchases, payments, expenses and journals are complete.
  3. Review general ledger and trial balance for unexpected balances.
  4. Compare receivables/payables reports with customer/vendor records.
  5. Review profit and loss and balance sheet totals; investigate and document differences.
  6. Have the responsible accountant approve period-end adjustments and statutory treatment.

6. GST Returns Workbench

Route: /gst-returns. The project documentation describes period-filtered GSTR-1 transaction and GSTR-3B reconciliation CSV workpapers generated from recorded invoices, purchase invoices and approved expenses.

Important limitation: These are review aids, not GST Portal upload files or tax advice. The app does not submit returns to GSTN. The current invoice model does not represent all statutory sections/transaction types (including HSN/SAC summaries, credit/debit notes, advances, exports/SEZ, reverse charge and e-commerce reporting). Input tax totals are not checked for ITC eligibility or reconciled against GSTR-2B. Reconcile all sections on the GST Portal and with your accountant before filing.

7. Installation, users, trial and license

Local setup (administrator)

  1. Install Node.js 24 and PostgreSQL.
  2. Run npm ci.
  3. Copy .env.example to .env; set DATABASE_URL and a unique SESSION_SECRET at least 32 bytes long.
  4. Run npx prisma generate and npx prisma migrate deploy.
  5. On a migrated empty database, open /signup to create the first administrator.
  6. Use npm run dev for local development.

Access and licensing

Users: /users; roles: /roles; audit logs: /audit-logs; company settings: /settings/company; license: /license. Grant only necessary permissions. The repository documents a seven-day trial and annual license model; confirm current commercial terms with Vikora. If activation fails, capture the exact error text with the code redacted and contact support.

The current free Render deployment is test-only. Do not store real customer or financial data there. Production requires an appropriate hosting plan, persistent storage, TLS, private database networking and automated backups.

8. Troubleshooting

Cannot sign in

  • Confirm the URL and account email.
  • Check account setup and trial/license state.
  • Capture the exact error without passwords, cookies or tokens.

Access denied

  • Ask the administrator to verify role and required permission.
  • Include the route and action attempted; do not bypass access controls.

Unexpected stock or report total

  • Check company, date range and financial period.
  • Review source transactions and recent changes.
  • Reconcile detail/ledger records before making an approved correction.

Import or save fails

  • Preserve the original file and record the exact validation message.
  • Check required fields and date/number formats in a copy.
  • Test with a small, non-sensitive sample before retrying.

App unavailable

  • Ask the administrator to check service health, deployment logs and database connectivity.
  • An idle free Render service may take time to wake.
  • Never expose database ports publicly or disable the firewall as a workaround.

Support reports should include module/route, steps, expected and actual result, exact error text, time/time zone and release/commit if known. Redact all confidential data.
