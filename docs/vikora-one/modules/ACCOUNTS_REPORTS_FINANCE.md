# Module Guide — Accounts, Ledgers, and Financial Reports

**Status:** General accounting support draft, not tax or accounting advice and not a verified product manual.

## Before entering a transaction

1. Identify the underlying event: sales invoice, supplier bill, receipt, payment, expense, or journal adjustment.
2. Use the approved source transaction where available to avoid recording the same event twice.
3. Confirm company/branch, period, date, account/ledger, reference, amount, currency, supporting document, and authorisation.
4. Verify debit/credit balance and the saved record's status.
5. Confirm the ledger effect using the installed version's documented workflow.

## Reports do not match

- Align company/branch, start/end date, currency, status/posting filters, and access scope.
- Compare report totals with the underlying transactions using identical filters.
- Inspect unposted, cancelled, duplicated, backdated, excluded, or reversed entries.
- Check opening balances and report-specific rules in the approved documentation.
- Record the difference and source records before changing anything.

Tax, payroll, statutory filings, period close, and accounting-policy choices must be checked with the customer's authorised accountant. Do not invent tax rules or assume a report is legally compliant.
