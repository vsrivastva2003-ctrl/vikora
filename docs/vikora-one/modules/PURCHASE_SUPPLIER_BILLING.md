# Module Guide — Purchases and Supplier Bills

**Status:** Generic workflow draft; edition-specific steps require verification.

## Procedure checklist

1. Confirm supplier, company/branch, date, permissions, and purchase process.
2. Search for an existing supplier bill using supplier invoice number/date and amount.
3. Match the approved purchase order, goods receipt, supplier invoice, and payment documents as required by company policy.
4. Check item codes, quantities received, units, rates, freight/other charges, and approved tax treatment.
5. Verify totals and supporting evidence before saving.
6. Reopen the saved record and confirm its status and reference.
7. Verify inventory and payable-ledger effects only according to the installed edition's documented posting rules.

## Troubleshooting

- **Bill mismatch:** Compare supplier invoice to approved order and receipt; identify quantity, rate, freight, or tax differences.
- **Possible duplicate:** Stop and search existing records before retrying.
- **Stock not updated:** Check receipt status and the documented integration/posting sequence.
- **Supplier balance differs:** Compare posted bills, credit/debit notes, payments, date range, and ledger filters.
