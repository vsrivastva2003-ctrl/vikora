VIKORA CUSTOMER GUIDE

Get started with confidence.

A straightforward introduction to evaluating, setting up and learning the Vikora product. The screens and available functions depend on your installed release, licence and user permissions.

01

Understand the product

Start with the product overview and confirm which modules are included in the edition you are evaluating. Ask for a guided demonstration if you want to see a particular workflow.

  • Review the available product modules.
  • Confirm licence type, user count and deployment method.
  • Discuss your business requirements before purchase.
02

Prepare your company setup

Before entering live information, review company details, financial year, branches and the user roles needed by your team. Keep access limited to each person's responsibilities.

  • Prepare master records and opening information.
  • Set up users and permissions.
  • Take a backup before major configuration changes.
03

Start with the workflow you need

Choose the relevant areas—such as sales and billing, purchase, inventory, accounts or reports—and learn that process first. Your installation may allow modules to be enabled according to your requirements.

04

Review records and reports

Check entries against the source documents and review the available summaries. Confirm figures with your own records before using them for tax, statutory or financial decisions.

05

Get help when you need it

For an installation, access or workflow issue, note the screen, action and error message. Remove sensitive customer or financial details before sharing screenshots with support.
