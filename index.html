STEP-BY-STEP OVERVIEW

How to get started with Vikora-One.

Use this as a customer orientation guide. The exact available screens and actions depend on your Vikora-One installation and assigned permissions.

01

Set your company context

Open Settings → Company Settings and review the organization details. Add your branches and financial year so records and reports use the right business context.

02

Add people and assign roles

Go to Settings → Users & Permissions. Create user accounts, select a role, and check which modules and actions the person needs. Apply a branch data scope where relevant.

03

Set up the records each team uses

Prepare customer and supplier records, products and inventory, project information, and other required business data before using the matching modules for live work.

04

Move a transaction through your workflow

For example, a team may manage a customer in CRM, create the business transaction in Sales, update relevant stock in Inventory, and review financial information in Accounts. Use the modules available to your role.

05

Review activity and reports

Use Projects for project work, HR & Payroll for people operations, and Reports & MIS to review available summaries and support decisions.
